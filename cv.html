Who am I?

I'm a hobbyist tinkerer and a researcher with passion for automation and perception. My work focuses on how state-of-the-art computer vision algorithms perform in complex, real-world environments and how we can optimize them to balance high performance with responsible & ethical application. Outside of research, I enjoy designing mechanical structures, running my home-lab, and spending time exploring nature.

Research Career

  • PostDoc @ Aalborg University & CAISA Jan 2024 - Now

    Working on trustworthy AI by translating ethical, societal, and legal demands into computable metrics. With a focus on ensuring evaluation properly assesses the alignment of AI systems with human values rather than just technical performance.

  • PhD Fellowship @ Aalborg University Jun 2020 - Oct 2023

    Researched how object-detection and segmentation algorithms perform in unconstrained real-world environments. Adapted models and training pipelines for increased robustness under continuous data drift.

  • Research Assistant @ Aalborg University Sep 2019 - Jun 2020

    Designed and implemented an extensive sensor array for an autonomous sewer-inspection robot, which needed to process and store large amounts of data in real-time on device.

Recent Projects

  • Standardization and Compliance with the AI-Act

    Member of the Danish AI standardization committee (S-855) and CEN/CENELEC JTC 21, contributing to standards and guidelines for responsible AI deployment, with a focus on evaluation methods and metrics for computer vision in support of the EU AI Act. Organize workshops connecting academia and industry on operationalizing responsible AI.

  • Long-term Thermal Drift

    Collected, curated, and analyzed one of the largest thermal object-detection datasets for long-term drift assessment. Organized open challenges integrating metadata to improve robustness across seasons. Maintain public leaderboards and ongoing analyses to track and characterize performance degradation over time.

  • Design of long-term data acquisition setups

    Designed and implemented vision sensor packages for autonomous systems in hazardous environments, including sewer-inspection robots, ROVs operating at depths exceeding 30 meters, and static long-term monitoring in ocean environments.
